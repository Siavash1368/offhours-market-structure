## 2

- Numbers: `ck_venue_weeks` = 1,082, `rec_adv_gap` = -13.4%, `rec_pass` = 7, `rec_reproducible` = 9
- Files: `reports/tables/a1_reconciliation.csv`, `reports/tables/checks.json`

## 3

- Numbers: `a1_first_week` = 2023-01-02, `a1_last_week` = 2026-09-14, `adv_first` = 2.6, `adv_growth_x` = 47, `adv_last` = 121, `t1_sh_dollars` = 62.7%, `t2_sh_shares` = 85.3%
- Files: `reports/tables/a1_summary.json`

- Numbers: `a3_events` = 7,108, `pl_gap` = +0.009, `pl_gap_hi` = +0.076, `pl_gap_lo` = -0.055
